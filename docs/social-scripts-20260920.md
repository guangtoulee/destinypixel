## 表达原则与拍摄口径

- 先让观众看懂一件与自己有关的事，再提 BaZi / Chinese astrology。保留东方文化来源，不要求观众先知道自己的原创动物名称。
- 人、道具和冲突承担画面变化。卡片或产品屏幕只在需要解释时出现，不用四张剪影、循环呼吸或长产品片头代替故事。
- 竖屏9:16；英文是实际口播，中文是意思与执行说明。下列时长是朗读初估，正式剪辑按录音调整，不能加速台词掩盖时间不够。
- 不展示真实姓名、生日、私人聊天或报告。录屏用明显的示例数据；有计算结果才录，不编造分数和第三方背书。
- 结尾有网站入口时用：**“Try it free at DestinyPixel. Link in bio.”** 没有入口时用：**“Find the free tool at DestinyPixel dot com.”** 二选一，不能空喊“点链接”。视频字幕显示 `destinypixel.com`；根据本条主题落到具体工具或文章。
- 只在演示生日卡时出现动物。名字以实际网站和卡面为准，不另起一套英文名，不把卡面角色当传统教义。

## Y2｜YouTube搜索型教程：How to Read Chinese Fortune Sticks Online

**时长预估：2分15秒左右；标题：Chinese Fortune Sticks Online: Ask, Draw, Check the Source**

> You searched a fortune-stick number and found three different explanations. Before choosing the one you like, check whether they are even explaining the same text.
>
> A numbered stick belongs to a collection. The number alone is not a universal reference across every temple or edition. Keep the collection name, the number, the opening line and any printed source. Compare those details before you compare meanings.
>
> If you are drawing online, start with one situation. “Will my whole life work out?” is difficult to use. “What should I clarify before accepting this role?” gives you something concrete to reflect on. For love, try “What do I need to ask before our next conversation?” rather than expecting a reading to reveal someone’s private thoughts.
>
> On DestinyPixel, choose a collection and a topic, then let one draw finish. You do not need either person’s birth details. Read the displayed text and the source note before requesting the optional AI explanation.
>
> There are different layers here. An original verse is a source text. A translation carries it into another language. A modern adaptation may reshape the imagery. An interpretation connects it with a question. Those can all be interesting, but they are not interchangeable.
>
> Our library combines selected traditional material with modern symbolic readings. It is not a complete transcription of every temple’s numbered collection. If your paper slip has different wording, the website’s AI explanation is about the website’s entry, not a verified explanation of the paper in your hand.
>
> After reading, write three short notes: the question you brought, the phrase that caught your attention and one thing you can check or discuss. For a work question, that could mean confirming the actual working hours. For a relationship question, it could mean asking for a conversation instead of making an assumption.
>
> If the reading does not fit, you do not have to force it. And drawing again until you like an answer does not establish a fact. Treat the experience as a reflective pause, then return to the real information in your situation.
