## en — Gui Hai Day Pillar (癸亥): The Galaxy Boar

Yin Water meets the Pig/Boar branch's principal Water. The Galaxy Boar closes the sixty-part cycle with an image of reflection, spacious imagination and returning to a particular world.

Gui Hai pairs 癸, Yin Water, with 亥, Pig/Boar, whose principal element is Water. Water repeats at these two levels. The Galaxy Boar imagines a night sky reflected in a dark sea. Its strength is spacious imagination; its tension is allowing the imagined whole to make the nearby world feel too small to enter.

> A reflection of the stars still belongs to a particular stretch of water. The Galaxy Boar asks how a vast inner image can return as one real word, encounter or beginning.

### 1. The sixtieth pair, not a spiritual ranking

Gui Hai is the 60th pair of the cycle. Gui is Yin Water and Hai is Pig/Boar with principal Water. The cycle then returns to Jia Zi; being last in the sequence does not make a pair more evolved or spiritually powerful.

The earlier and current Galaxy Boar share reflected stars. This is original cosmic imagery, not evidence of clairvoyance or a special connection to the universe. Repeated Water alone cannot establish a complete chart's strength.

### 2. Room for an image not yet explained

This portrait values the moment before an impression has become a conclusion: a dreamlike scene, a story ending with space around it, or a question that changes as you sit with it. Imagination can hold more than one possible meaning.

The tension is making the actual world seem inadequate beside that spaciousness. A small beginning may feel unworthy of the image, so nothing is begun. If the portrait resonates, a partial expression can be a return from imagination rather than a betrayal of it.


### 3. A shared silence can also have a beginning

Imagine sitting together after a film that moved you. You have many impressions but cannot find the complete explanation. Your companion asks what stayed with you, and you almost say nothing.

Naming one image is enough to begin: the empty platform, a colour or the last glance. In this reading, closeness can grow from fragments offered honestly. Another person need not enter the entire inner sea in order to meet you at its edge.


### 4. Let the large image take one small form

A writer imagines a whole world but cannot begin the book. Writing a single room with one object and one person gives the world a point of entry. Some of the original vision changes as it becomes concrete.

This can illuminate art, writing or any work beginning with an impression. It does not promise effortless success or money attracted by thought. The question is how imagination can participate in making, where form inevitably selects some possibilities and leaves others open.


### 5. Gui Hai and Ren Zi: reflection and a moving horizon

Both have Water Day Masters and principal Water in their branches. Gui Hai has Yin Water and Pig/Boar; Ren Zi has Yang Water and Rat. Shared Water does not erase these different components or establish a complete reading.

The Tsunami Rat explores many outward possibilities and a chosen shore. The Galaxy Boar explores many inward meanings and a first expression. These are narrative directions, not a fixed division between active and passive people.

### 6. Bring back one fragment

Choose an image or question that has occupied your mind. Give one fragment a concrete form today: a sentence, a sketch or a question asked aloud. Let it be smaller than the whole.

Notice what happens when the fragment meets paper or another person. The practice makes a return possible. The sea need not be emptied for one reflection to become visible.


### 7. The end of the cycle is still one day pair

A Pig/Boar year does not establish Gui Hai. Use the full date and keep an unknown time unknown. Other pillars, season and day-boundary conventions are needed for fuller context.

The Hong Kong Observatory source supports the calendar framework; the philosophy reference explains traditional generating and controlling cycles. ## zh — 癸亥日柱：星河灵猪

### 2. 给尚未解释的画面留位置

这幅肖像珍惜印象还没成为结论的时刻：近似梦境的场景，留着余地的故事结尾，或想了一会儿以后已经变化的问题。想象可以容纳不止一种意义。

张力是与那份宽广相比，现实世界似乎总不够好。小开端配不上大画面，于是什么也没开始。如果意象与你相近，一部分表达也可以是从想象中归来，不必被视为背叛想象。


### 3. 共同的安静，也可以有开头

设想一起看完触动自己的电影。感受很多，却找不到完整解释。对方问留下了什么，你差一点说没有。

说出一个画面就能开始：空站台，一种颜色，或最后的回望。在这套读法中，亲近可以从真实递出的碎片生长。别人不必进入整片内海，才有机会在岸边遇见你。


### 4. 让大画面先有一个小形状

作者想出整个世界，却迟迟无法动笔。先写一个房间、一件物品和一个人，世界就有了入口。变得具体时，原来的想象也会改变一部分。

它可以照见艺术、写作或任何从印象开始的工作，不承诺轻松成功或靠想法吸引金钱。问题是想象怎样参与创作；形式总会选择一部分可能，也把另一部分留着。


### 5. 癸亥与壬子：倒影，与流动的地平线

两者都以水为日主，地支本气也属水。癸亥为阴水配亥猪，壬子为阳水配子鼠。同属水不抹去组成差别，也不等于完整解释。

惊涛灵鼠探索向外的许多可能与一处选定的岸；星河灵猪探索向内的许多意义与第一次表达。这是叙事方向，不把人固定成主动与被动两类。

选一个占据心里的画面或问题，今天给其中一小部分具体形状：一句话、一张草图，或一个问出口的问题。允许它小于全部。
