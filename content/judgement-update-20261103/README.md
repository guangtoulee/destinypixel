## What the reader gets

The existing article already connects the trumpet with an answer, and it already distinguishes Justice from Judgement and Judgement from the World. It did not say where Rachel Pollack places the call after the Sun, how she separates Judgement’s rebirth from Death, or that a response rewrites circumstances people share. Those three points are now in the Chinese article once each.

1. After「谁在发声，谁在回答」: the call meets an inner desire and an outer signal, and the cross on the banner joins ordinary time with a sense of the eternal. This is Pollack’s symbolic reading, not a claim that the reader has been chosen.
