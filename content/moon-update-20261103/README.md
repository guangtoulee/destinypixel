## What the reader gets

The existing article already describes the crayfish, the dog and wolf, and the path between the towers, and it already says a Moon draw is not evidence that someone is deceiving the reader. It did not give Pollack's reading of those three images. The Chinese article now does, once each.

1. At the end of「沿着这条路慢慢看」, after the existing image description: the crayfish is more alien than the animals, Pollack's report of Waite's “deeper than beasts”, the half-ashore retreat, and fear that has not fully formed. Marked as symbolic reading, not a monster and not a diagnosis.
2. In the same section, one sentence only: relative to Strength, wildness is fuller at the Moon, where the animals howl, while Strength guides the animal. The emperor / superego / id passage is not used.
3. In「Waite 的关键词是反射的光」, after the path-as-exit paragraph and before the existing divinatory-vocabulary warning: the Star has no road back, the path through the towers is an entrance, and the road is still there after fear settles. This does not turn the Moon into deception or a plot.
