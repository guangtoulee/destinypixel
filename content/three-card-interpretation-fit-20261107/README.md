# Three-card interpretation fit, 2026-11-07

This revises the existing `/journal/how-to-read-three-card-tarot`, not the separate `how-to-connect-three-tarot-cards` guide or the open journal PR. English and Simplified Chinese gain one section after `connect`. The original eight sections, paragraphs, tables, steps, source lists, internal links, anchors, metadata and actions remain exact. Russian and Traditional Chinese retain their complete earlier editions. Only the authored EN/ZH modification dates become November 7; publication and the other two editions remain October 2.

The new material answers a specific reader problem: how to question an interpretation without treating either reassurance or discomfort as proof. A deliberately invented newsletter scenario keeps one question, the actual tool's three position roles and three chosen upright cards fixed. The table withdraws both an unsupported promise of help and an unsupported verdict of incapacity, leaving a provisional planning prompt that may itself be revised or declined. This is an original teaching exercise, not a user's reading, a new tool preset or a scientific test of divination.
