## What the reader gets

The existing article already close-reads the image, records Waite, reads upright and reversed change, compares nearby cards, and keeps both cases, the practice and the misreading note. It did not give Rachel Pollack’s reading of the snake, the jackal-headed figure and the sphinx, the step from the Hermit to the World, or her short note that a change in circumstances may not be the querent’s responsibility. Those three points are now in the Chinese article once each.

1. After the last paragraph of「绕着轮看一圈，再看四个角」, with no new heading: one paragraph reads the descending snake, the rising jackal-headed figure and the sword-bearing sphinx as Pollack’s picture of death and rebirth coexisting. Wheel chapter page 118. It is her author symbolism, not a settled Egyptological fact, and it does not predict illness, death, success or failure. A short following paragraph notes that early Christians used the four winged figures for the four Evangelists, which is why they hold books. Wheel chapter page 116. That note does not assign tarot a religion and does not use scripture for prediction. The medieval admonition passage is omitted.
2. After「把相近的牌义分清楚」, a new subsection titled exactly「从隐士的退后一步，到世界的整合」(`depth-sequence`): the Hermit steps back and can see the whole wheel; this page is a symbolic overview; the World lives that integration out. Wheel chapter pages 115 and 114. Stepping back does not require seclusion. The Hermit links to its existing page only, because that page has no `depth-sequence` anchor. The World links to its existing page only. Justice links to the existing `depth-sequence` anchor. This does not override Waite.
3. After the reversed paragraphs in「逆位 不顺、过量和反复，都值得分开看」, with no new heading: a change in circumstances may not be the querent’s responsibility. Wheel chapter page 120. The company merger with job loss, and a relationship ending, are marked as book examples, not predictions and not blame. Upright may be read as adapting, without guaranteeing that a change must be meaningful and without telling anyone to get over it or to drop an appeal. Reversed may be resisting a change that has already happened, or a previously passive person beginning a new awareness. How one responds afterwards is not the same as having caused the change. Waite’s reversed wording on increase, abundance and excess, and the distinction that an external delay is not the same as repeating old methods, stay verbatim. Pollack supplements those layers. The card does not replace labor advice, a legal process, or support.
