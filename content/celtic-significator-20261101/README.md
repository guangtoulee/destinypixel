## Attribution and original teaching contribution

Pollack's discussion is identified as her modern reading method, not a universal tarot rule. The preselected reference in Waite's procedure is distinguished from the site's drawn positions 1 (Present situation) and 7 (Your approach). The current site's Celtic preset has ten positions and no separate significator slot; this does not describe every possible custom table or tarot tradition.
