## Reader question and contribution

“Does the Two of Pentacles always mean I am overloaded?” The earlier guide already mentions Waite's recreation association, but nearly all its applications concern schedules and capacity. The new original storytelling-game example demonstrates a different, fact-dependent use: responding to change may be part of enjoyable participation. A three-step paper exercise keeps the card upright, checks observable adjustments and participants' stated choices, and allows either perspective to be declined. Play is not required to become productivity work; a cheerful image cannot establish another person's feelings. Earlier advice becomes relevant again if actual commitments are displaced.
