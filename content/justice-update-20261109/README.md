## What the reader gets

The existing article already close-reads the image, records Waite, reads upright and reversed fairness, compares nearby cards, and keeps both cases, the practice and the misreading note. It did not give Rachel Pollack’s reading of the uncovered eyes, why Justice sits between the Wheel and the Hanged Man in her sequence, or her short reversed possibility. Those three points are now in the Chinese article once each.

1. After the last paragraph of「一只手衡量，一只手持剑」, with no new heading and in one short paragraph: uncovered eyes are Pollack’s “law of justice in the soul,” not a social-court rule; the upright sword and the scales are her symbols of understanding and action. Justice chapter pages 121–122. No Themis or Justitia history, no color reading, no claim that a decision can never be undone, and no case prediction.
2. After「把相近的牌义分清楚」, a new subsection titled exactly「为什么正义夹在命运之轮与倒吊人之间」(`depth-sequence`): the Wheel observes a life, Justice understands and responds through responsibility, and the Hanged Man is acceptance and preparation. An earthquake example keeps the material limit: experience can influence a person without meaning that person caused it or deserved harm, and responsibility is not a moral accusation. Justice chapter pages 123–124 and Hanged Man chapter pages 128–129, kept as separate sources. The Wheel links to its existing page only. The Hanged Man links to the existing `depth-sequence` anchor. This does not override Waite. No midpoint numerology.
3. After the reversed paragraphs in「逆位 先检查秤有没有被手指压住」, with no new heading and in one short paragraph: a person may be unwilling to face certain facts, or may actually have met unfairness. Justice chapter page 127. The original ending about not proving fault or another person’s unfairness stays verbatim. Appeal, help and protecting one’s rights stay in the misreading note. No diagnosis, no accusatory monologue, and no replacement of a legal process or support.
